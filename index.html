Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Who We Are

Grabbit is an online platform that helps users discover and compare deals across multiple categories including products, hotels, cafes, cars, flights, courses, and event tickets. We are currently in our launch phase and building partnerships with major retailers and service providers.

2. Information We Collect

We do not currently collect any personal data from visitors to this website. We do not store your name, email address, or payment information. In the future, if we introduce an account registration system, we will update this policy accordingly and ask for your consent.

When you use our Contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent directly to our support email via Formspree. We do not store this data on any server we own.

3. Cookies

This website may use basic browser cookies to improve your browsing experience. We do not use cookies to track purchases or personal behaviour on third-party websites.

4. Affiliate Disclosure

Grabbit is an affiliate marketing platform. This means that when you click on a product or deal link and make a purchase on the retailer's website, Grabbit may receive a small commission from that retailer. This commission comes at absolutely no extra cost to you.

We are currently in the process of establishing official affiliate partnerships with various retailers and service providers. All confirmed partner relationships will be disclosed clearly on this page.

5. Third-Party Websites

When you click "Grab Now" on any listing, you will be redirected to a third-party retailer's website. Grabbit is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We recommend reviewing their individual privacy policies before making a purchase.

6. Prices and Accuracy

All prices, descriptions, and availability shown on Grabbit are for reference purposes only. Actual prices may vary on the retailer's website. Grabbit does not guarantee the accuracy of any pricing information displayed on this platform.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data at this time, there is minimal privacy risk to visitors. All actual transactions and data collection occur on our partners' secure, encrypted platforms.

8. Changes to This Policy

As Grabbit grows and we add new features, we will update this Privacy Policy. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
